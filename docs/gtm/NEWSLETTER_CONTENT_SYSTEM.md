# Newsletter Content System

Status: proposed

## Role

The newsletter is the free distribution layer for approved PSE intelligence and the recurring public introduction to CHAP.

## Issue structure

1. What changed
2. Why it matters
3. CHAP’s question of the week
4. Preview of the full Context Hub analysis
5. Context Hub CTA
6. Relevant assessment CTA

## Publishing pipeline

```text
source.detected
→ change.classified
→ impact.drafted
→ specialist.reviewed
→ intelligence.approved
→ hub.published
→ newsletter.derived
→ editorial.approved
→ subscriber.notified
```

Newsletter automation may assemble only from approved records. It may not promote an AI inference into authoritative guidance.

## Risk-based review

| Content | Required gate |
| --- | --- |
| General industry trend | Editorial review |
| Official reminder | Source verification and editorial approval |
| Rate, threshold, deadline, or effective-date change | Payroll-specialist verification |
| Legal interpretation | Qualified expert review |
| Conflicting sources | Hold and escalate |
| AI inference | Label clearly; never treat as authority |

## Data and consent

Subscriber capture may request role, company size, geography, and interests when the purpose is disclosed. Behavioral segmentation must be proportionate and explainable. Provider selection, retention, unsubscribe, and consent language require approval before implementation.

## CHAP teaser rule

Every CHAP teaser must link to an approved intelligence record or approved product demonstration. The teaser must not imply that the free email includes member-only analysis.

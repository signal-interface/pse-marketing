## Route-state policy

Every public route must declare one state:

- **Current:** supported now and demonstrable.
- **Pilot:** available only through an approved engagement.
- **Planned:** explanatory page with no availability promise.
- **Illustrative:** depicts intended experience and is visibly labeled.

Empty route shells must not be deployed solely to make the navigation look complete.

## Compatibility positioning

Use “PSE + platform” pages rather than replacement claims:

- PSE + ADP
- PSE + UKG
- PSE + Dayforce
- PSE + Workday

Dedicated comparison content may address spreadsheets, manual controls, periodic assessments, or generic AI assistants when claims are evidence-backed.

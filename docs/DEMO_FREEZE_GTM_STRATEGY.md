# PSE Demo-Freeze GTM Strategy

Status: Proposed canonical GTM operating strategy  
Owner: PSE / Signal Interface  
Applies to: `signal-interface/pse-marketing`

## 1. Executive intent

PSE marketing is intentionally being prepared before the product demo is frozen so that the demo freeze becomes the trigger for GTM finalization, not the starting point for marketing strategy.

The repository should therefore maintain two distinct states:

1. **Pre-freeze preparation** — build the positioning, content foundations, channels, website structure, lead-capture mechanics, analytics plan, and launch asset templates without making unsupported product claims.
2. **Post-freeze activation** — bind every external claim, campaign, CTA, qualification step, sales asset, and measurement event to the frozen demonstration and approved pilot offer.

The goal is to move from demo freeze to launch readiness quickly without reopening category strategy, changing the core narrative, or introducing new product concepts during the launch sprint.

## 2. Strategic principle

The frozen demo is the commercial reference implementation for PSE.

It is not the full technical ceiling of PSE, but it is the authoritative external reference for what the market can see, understand, evaluate, and buy.

All marketing claims must map to one of three states:

- **Available now** — demonstrable and supported in the frozen product experience.
- **Available in pilot** — delivered through the approved pilot scope, including controlled manual or human-assisted steps where explicitly disclosed.
- **Planned capability** — part of the roadmap and clearly labeled as not currently available.

No marketing asset may blur these states.

## 3. GTM objective

The immediate GTM objective is to create one repeatable commercial path from payroll-governance awareness to a qualified PSE pilot opportunity.

The initial operating loop is:

```text
One target customer profile
→ one urgent payroll-governance problem
→ one primary traffic source
→ one primary value-entry experience
→ one qualification path
→ one trust meeting
→ one paid diagnostic or pilot offer
→ one repeatable delivery handoff
→ one measurable customer result
```

PSE may present a professional platform with multiple capabilities, but the GTM front door must remain simple enough for a buyer to understand and act on immediately.

## 4. Pre-freeze strategy: queue the system

Before the demo is frozen, the marketing repository should prepare the infrastructure and reusable materials that do not depend on final product behavior.

### 4.1 Positioning foundation

Maintain and refine:

- PSE category position
- system-agnostic payroll-governance narrative
- governed AI and human-approval boundaries
- platform-level differentiation
- what PSE is and is not
- approved terminology
- target buyer hypotheses
- primary problem hypotheses

These materials may remain provisional until the demo freeze, but they should be structured so final wording can be updated quickly.

### 4.2 Content foundation

Prepare content around durable customer problems rather than volatile feature descriptions:

- payroll governance
- payroll operating risk
- compliance controls
- audit readiness
- exception management
- AI governance in payroll
- evidence and traceability
- system-agnostic oversight
- control ownership and remediation

Content should educate the market and build category authority without claiming that unfinished functionality is available.

### 4.3 Channel foundation

Prepare the channels and operating templates for:

- founder-led LinkedIn authority content
- SEO pillar pages and supporting clusters
- owned email audience and nurture
- direct relationship and referral outreach
- launch announcements
- pilot recruitment

The primary launch traffic source should be selected at freeze. The current default hypothesis is founder-led LinkedIn, with SEO as the compounding secondary channel.

### 4.4 Conversion foundation

Prepare the reusable conversion infrastructure:

- landing-page templates
- demo or diagnostic request form
- source and campaign attribution fields
- lead database
- email notifications
- meeting-scheduling handoff
- qualification questionnaire template
- follow-up email templates
- pipeline-stage definitions
- pilot interest and waitlist capture

The final qualification fields and primary CTA remain provisional until the product and offer are frozen.

### 4.5 Asset foundation

Prepare templates and production workflows for:

- product demo video
- sales deck
- executive one-pager
- product screenshots
- use-case pages
- feature pages
- comparison pages
- launch campaign posts
- email sequence
- pilot brief
- FAQ
- objection-handling guide
- customer proof package

Templates may be designed and partially populated before freeze, but no asset is final until verified against the frozen demo.

## 5. Demo-freeze definition

Marketing activation may begin only after a formal demo-freeze decision confirms the following.

### 5.1 Product scope locked

- primary buyer and user are identified
- primary problem is defined
- demo start and end states are fixed
- modules and workflows shown are fixed
- product terminology is approved
- unavailable features are removed, hidden, or clearly labeled
- data used in the demo is approved
- known limitations are documented

### 5.2 Product truth locked

- every visible claim can be demonstrated
- live functionality is distinguished from illustrative data
- manual or human-assisted pilot steps are disclosed
- compliance and legal boundaries are approved
- AI output boundaries and human-review requirements are approved
- screenshots and recordings can be reproduced reliably

### 5.3 Commercial offer locked

- paid entry offer or pilot is defined
- deliverables are fixed
- duration is fixed
- customer prerequisites are fixed
- acceptance and exclusion criteria are fixed
- pricing or pricing approach is approved
- delivery owner and handoff process are defined
- success measures are defined

### 5.4 Operational readiness locked

- demo environment is stable
- lead capture is functional
- notification routing is verified
- meeting scheduling is available
- qualification workflow is documented
- sales follow-up ownership is assigned
- pilot intake can begin without improvising the process

A demo is not considered frozen merely because development pauses. It is frozen when product, claims, offer, and operating handoff are all stable enough to support external traffic.

## 6. Post-freeze activation sprint

The demo freeze triggers a time-boxed GTM finalization sprint. The purpose of the sprint is execution and validation, not reopening product strategy.

### Workstream 1: canonical narrative

Finalize:

- one-sentence product definition
- primary value proposition
- primary customer problem
- platform narrative
- approved capability descriptions
- primary CTA
- pilot offer language
- available-now, pilot, and planned labels

Deliverable: one canonical messaging source used by the website, deck, video, social content, email, and sales materials.

### Workstream 2: website binding

Update the site so every important claim maps to the frozen demo.

Required changes:

- homepage hero aligned to the primary outcome
- platform page aligned to actual workflow
- use cases mapped to demonstrated capabilities
- screenshots captured from the frozen environment
- unfinished functionality labeled or removed
- primary CTA standardized
- lead form aligned to qualification needs
- confirmation and follow-up experience aligned to the sales process

Deliverable: production website ready to receive qualified traffic.

### Workstream 3: demo and sales assets

Produce:

- short product overview video
- full guided demo
- executive sales deck
- one-page platform brief
- paid diagnostic or pilot brief
- FAQ and objection guide
- follow-up email sequence

Deliverable: a consistent buyer journey from first awareness through pilot discussion.

### Workstream 4: traffic activation

Finalize the 1–1–1 launch motion:

- **One primary traffic source**
- **One conversion mechanism**
- **One initial commercial delivery path**

Recommended initial configuration:

- Traffic: founder-led LinkedIn authority content
- Conversion: qualification form followed by a diagnostic trust meeting
- Delivery: fixed-scope Payroll Governance Diagnostic or approved PSE pilot

SEO, email, referrals, and other channels support the motion but do not replace the primary controlled experiment.

### Workstream 5: pipeline activation

Finalize the lifecycle:

```text
Visitor
→ engaged visitor
→ identified lead
→ marketing-qualified lead
→ sales-qualified lead
→ meeting scheduled
→ meeting completed
→ diagnostic or pilot proposed
→ accepted / declined / nurture
→ pilot active
→ expansion candidate
```

For every stage define:

- entry criteria
- owner
- required action
- response-time expectation
- exit criteria
- reason codes
- measurement event

Deliverable: no lead enters the system without a defined next action and accountable owner.

### Workstream 6: instrumentation

Implement a shared event taxonomy, including at minimum:

```text
traffic_arrived
content_viewed
lead_asset_started
lead_asset_completed
resource_downloaded
chap_question_asked
email_captured
qualification_started
qualification_submitted
meeting_requested
meeting_scheduled
meeting_completed
pilot_proposed
pilot_accepted
pilot_declined
```

Capture where available:

- session identifier
- contact identifier
- source
- medium
- campaign
- content asset
- target persona
- company size
- payroll complexity
- primary problem
- lifecycle stage
- timestamp

Deliverable: PSE can connect campaigns and content to meetings, proposals, and pilot outcomes.

## 7. Initial GTM hypothesis

The initial hypothesis to validate after freeze is:

### Ideal customer profile

Payroll leaders and adjacent finance or compliance stakeholders operating complex, multi-jurisdiction payroll environments where control evidence, exception visibility, audit readiness, or AI governance is inadequate.

### Urgent problem

The organization cannot reliably see, prioritize, and prove control over payroll operating risk before issues affect employees, filings, funding, or executive confidence.

### Primary promise

PSE helps payroll leaders understand where payroll can fail, what requires human attention, and what evidence demonstrates that the process was controlled.

### Primary offer

A fixed-scope Payroll Governance Diagnostic or approved pilot that produces:

- operating-risk map
- control and evidence assessment
- exception and ownership review
- prioritized remediation plan
- executive findings memo
- recommended pilot or implementation path

This hypothesis must be validated with real buyer conversations and pilot outcomes. It should not be treated as permanent merely because it is documented.

## 8. Role of CHAP and interactive tools

CHAP, estimators, calculators, reports, and downloadable resources are supporting GTM mechanisms.

Their roles are to:

- demonstrate governed intelligence
- provide immediate value
- educate the buyer
- capture intent
- identify the buyer's problem
- route qualified buyers toward the primary offer

They must not fragment the market narrative or make PSE appear to be only a compliance chatbot, calculator, or content site.

The platform remains the product. Interactive tools are proof, education, and qualification surfaces within the broader GTM system.

## 9. Decision rules

### Before demo freeze

Do:

- prepare templates and infrastructure
- build problem-led content
- test messaging hypotheses privately
- improve lead-capture reliability
- define measurement requirements
- document buyer questions and objections

Do not:

- launch unsupported product claims
- build campaigns around unstable features
- finalize screenshots from changing interfaces
- create multiple competing primary offers
- scale paid traffic into an unfinished conversion path
- treat provisional copy as canonical

### After demo freeze

Do:

- finalize the canonical narrative
- bind claims to the demo
- standardize the CTA
- activate the pipeline
- begin controlled distribution
- measure each funnel stage
- use buyer feedback to improve conversion and delivery

Do not:

- reopen the product vision during launch finalization
- add unrelated campaigns or personas
- expand channels before the primary loop is measurable
- promise roadmap capabilities as current
- allow leads to remain without ownership or follow-up

## 10. Launch gates

Public launch requires all gates below to pass.

### Product gate

- frozen demo is stable
- claims are demonstrable
- limitations are documented
- security and compliance blockers are resolved or explicitly gated

### Narrative gate

- canonical messaging approved
- website, video, deck, and sales language match
- current, pilot, and planned states are unambiguous

### Offer gate

- pilot or diagnostic scope approved
- intake requirements approved
- commercial terms approved
- delivery owner confirmed

### Pipeline gate

- forms and notifications verified
- meeting scheduling verified
- qualification workflow active
- follow-up sequence ready
- lifecycle stages and owners configured

### Measurement gate

- source attribution captured
- conversion events verified
- baseline dashboard available
- weekly review owner assigned

### Proof gate

- demo evidence is credible
- statistics and claims have approved sources
- illustrative examples are labeled
- customer proof is used only with permission

Failure of a gate pauses only the dependent launch activity. It does not automatically reopen unrelated strategy decisions.

## 11. Success metrics

The first phase should prioritize learning and conversion quality over raw reach.

Primary metrics:

- qualified visits by source
- qualification completion rate
- meeting-request rate
- meeting-show rate
- qualified-opportunity rate
- pilot proposal rate
- pilot acceptance rate
- time from lead to next action
- cost per qualified opportunity where applicable

Secondary metrics:

- content engagement
- email-list growth
- resource downloads
- repeat visits
- CHAP or tool engagement
- organic search growth

Business proof metrics:

- risks identified
- evidence gaps surfaced
- control ownership clarified
- remediation actions accepted
- preparation time reduced
- issues detected earlier
- executive confidence or audit readiness improved

Vanity metrics should not override evidence that the system is producing qualified conversations and measurable customer value.

## 12. Governance and repository boundary

This repository owns active PSE marketing execution, including:

- PSE positioning and campaign materials
- website copy and conversion surfaces
- launch-specific assets
- PSE content plans
- campaign measurement specifications
- PSE sales-enablement materials

Reusable cross-product GTM templates, abstractions, automation components, and channel-routing patterns may later be promoted into Faraday Studio after Studio core is scaffolded and the asset has demonstrated reuse beyond PSE.

Do not prematurely move active PSE campaign work out of this repository.

## 13. Operating cadence after activation

### Weekly GTM review

Review:

- traffic by source
- conversion by stage
- lead quality
- response times
- meetings and outcomes
- objections and unanswered questions
- content performance
- product or offer friction

Each review should end with one constrained improvement to the highest-leverage bottleneck.

### Monthly message review

Review:

- what buyers repeat back
- which claims create confusion
- which problem produces urgency
- which assets support trust
- which features are being overemphasized
- whether the primary promise remains accurate

### Quarterly strategic review

Ask:

- Is the ICP still correct?
- Is the primary problem still urgent?
- Is the offer producing measurable value?
- Where does the funnel break?
- What depends too heavily on the founder?
- What should be subtracted before another channel, persona, or offer is added?

## 14. Canonical sequence

```text
Marketing foundation queued
→ demo frozen
→ product truth and commercial offer locked
→ canonical narrative approved
→ website and sales assets finalized
→ pipeline and instrumentation activated
→ controlled traffic launched
→ qualified meetings converted
→ pilot outcomes captured
→ proof strengthens marketing
→ scale only after the loop is repeatable
```

## 15. Final strategic statement

PSE will not wait until the demo is frozen to begin thinking about marketing. It will prepare the marketing operating system in advance while deliberately withholding final claims and launch activation until the product reference point is stable.

Once the demo is frozen, PSE will execute a focused finalization sprint that converts the prepared foundation into a measurable GTM pipeline. Every campaign, CTA, content asset, qualification step, and sales promise will map to the demonstrated professional product platform and its approved commercial offer.

The objective is speed without improvisation, breadth without confusion, and professional market readiness without marketing beyond product truth.

# CHAP AI Naming Standard

**Canonical expansion:** CHAP AI = **Compliance, Helpdesk & Automation Platform**.

## Public-facing rule

On the first substantive CHAP reference on a page, product introduction, or customer-facing document, expand the name as:

> **CHAP AI (Compliance, Helpdesk & Automation Platform)**

After that first introduction, **CHAP AI** may be used without repeating the expansion.

## Consistency

Do not introduce alternate CHAP acronym expansions in active PSE marketing materials. Historical artifacts may retain prior text for evidentiary integrity.

This naming clarification does not change CHAP's product scope, governed boundaries, or the claims that may be made on the marketing site.

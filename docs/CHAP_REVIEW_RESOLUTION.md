# CHAP Review Resolution

Codex review feedback from the initial CHAP naming change identified that the per-page rule was broader than the implementation.

Resolution:
- dedicated `/chap-ai` hero and metadata now expand CHAP as **Compliance, Helpdesk & Automation Platform**;
- broader service pages expand CHAP in their first CHAP-focused explanatory section;
- the naming standard now defines what counts as a substantive introduction so incidental labels and links do not create false noncompliance.

No runtime behavior, product boundary, or governance claim changes are introduced.

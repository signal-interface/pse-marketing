# PSE Content Strategy

Status: Planning document only

## Primary Audience

- Payroll leaders
- HR operations leaders
- Finance and controllership leaders
- Compliance leaders
- Operations executives

## Core Message

If you are running AI in payroll, PSE provides the governance framework.

## Messaging Hierarchy

1. Payroll is a critical control environment.
2. Payroll teams are adopting automation faster than governance is maturing.
3. PSE provides the framework to govern payroll intelligence, compliance, and operational controls.
4. Human approval, auditability, and traceability remain central.

## Content Pillars

- Payroll governance
- AI in payroll
- Compliance operations
- Payroll controls
- Audit readiness
- Exception management
- System-agnostic payroll intelligence

## Content Formats

- Governance guides
- Payroll risk explainers
- AI policy templates
- Case studies
- Compliance checklists
- Executive briefs
- Product framework pages

## Primary CTA

Request a discovery conversation.

## Secondary CTAs

- Join pilot queue
- Download framework
- Review governance approach

## Content Boundary

PSE content should not imply that PSE processes payroll, files taxes, replaces HCM systems, or provides legal advice.

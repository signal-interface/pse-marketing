# PSE Payroll Intelligence: editorial governance and quarterly release gates
Status: DRAFT — 2026-11-03. No production publication authorization.

## Scope, positioning and authority
Educational marketing resource published under the PSE domain. The 15 subjects are based on coverage themes observed in photos of the historical *Payroll Source* contents; content must be original. Governance and independent assurance only: the marketing site does not calculate wages, remit taxes, alter a payroll system, or provide individualized client advice. The ratified infrastructure-first PSE product sequence remains separate.

## Three-level publication model
1. Domain coverage directory: original taxonomy and intended topics only, never characterized as legal advice or complete jurisdictional coverage.
2. Control-plan editorial draft: an assurance question, checklist, evidence suggestions, starting citation and explicit limitation. It is not a validated rule statement.
3. Verified article: each specific factual legal assertion individually tied to authoritative sources, with jurisdiction, applicability and effective-date metadata, named reviewer/approver, revision record, and authorization to publish.

## Required verified-article schema (future CMS or content files)
- content_id: stable identifier; title, domain_id, owner, review_state
- jurisdiction: country, state, locality, and affected worker/employer profile
- source_register: title, agency, exact URL, canonical citation, publication revision, access/check date
- applicability: effective_from, effective_to or open-ended, exceptions, source confidence and ambiguity notes
- rule_and_control: original summary, threshold values and units, employer obligation, control objective, expected evidence
- governance: subject expert, approver, legal/tax escalation, decision date, next review due, content version
- changelog: changed field, before/after explanation, source link, correction urgency and history link

## Update triggers
Quarterly editorial review targets: 2027 Jan 15, Apr 15, Jul 15, Oct 15 (not promises of live publishing).
Interim review triggers: emergency agency correction, new or amended federal/state/local law, court decision materially affecting applicability, deadline update, tax-year thresholds, source withdrawal. These triggers create review work only. Publishing is approval-gated.

## Release protocol
- Create quarter-specific inventory and differences against last approved snapshot.
- Distinguish announced/proposed, enacted, effective, superseded, and withdrawn material.
- Verify primary authority and check all relevant effective-date windows; no blanket 2026-to-2027 carry-forward.
- Obtain independent subject-matter check, then approve legal/tax-sensitive copy where applicable.
- Confirm regional overlays rather than assuming federal guidance applies universally.
- Check code compilation, governance boundary lint, source links, mobile layout, accessibility, and no secrets or employee data.
- Version and archive approved public content with an immutable release manifest and post-publication correction route.
- Only after review, obtain explicit approval for navigation/indexing and deployment. Domain pages remain `noindex` during staging.

# PSE Payroll Intelligence — State Source Briefings Batch 04 (2026-Q4)

**Scope:** Original PSE marketing education only. **Date checked:** November 3, 2026. **Coverage:** introductory state references expand from 19 to 23 of 51 states/DC jurisdictions. This is not a comprehensive regulatory matrix, operational rule registry or employer-specific legal opinion.

| State | Briefing | Official source and pinpoint | Exceptions/currency concerns |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alaska | Final wages on discharge or resignation | [Alaska DOL Wage and Hour FAQ Q18](https://www.labor.alaska.gov/lss/whfaq.htm); Alaska Stat. §23.05.140 | Employer termination generally within three working days; resignation next payday at least three working days later. Exclude weekends/holidays; confirm separation type and earned compensation |
| Arizona | Final wages | [Arizona Legislature A.R.S. §23-353](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/23/00353.htm) | Discharged employees paid within seven working days or end of next regular pay period, whichever sooner. Quitting employees paid no later than normal payday for terminating pay period. Verify payment method and statute version |
| Idaho | Final wages and written demand | [Idaho DOL Labor Laws FAQ](https://www.labor.idaho.gov/businesses/labor-laws/labor-laws-faq/); Idaho Code §45-606 | Due sooner of next payday or 10 days after separation (weekends/holidays excluded); written request can accelerate to 48 hours after receipt, excluding weekends/holidays. Director can grant extensions and other wage categories differ |
| Nevada | Final wages and distinct penalties | [Nevada Legislature NRS chapter 608](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/NRS/NRS-608.html), §§608.020, .030, .040 | Discharge and certain nonworking status: accrued wages due immediately. Quit: next regular payday or seven days, whichever earlier. Three-day provision for discharged wages in §608.040 concerns *penalty timing*, not a three-day general payment due date |

# Claim Evidence Intake Contract — Editorial Only
Prepared: 2026-11-03. Related PR: #34. Release decision: HOLD.

## Purpose
The PSE marketing knowledge framework has 15 domain briefings and one separate federal baseline. Individual draft claims are identified by `src/lib/payroll-intelligence-claim-inventory.ts`. The new `src/lib/payroll-intelligence-review.ts` establishes a separate evidence-intake contract that requires source pinpoints, authority revision, applicability and documented human review before a claim can move from draft source mapping into editorial consideration.

**No publication function or approval credential is provided.** `reviewed-for-editorial-consideration` is neither legal sign-off nor a public publishing decision.

## Claim workflow
1. `needs-source`: initial state. Candidate article-level citations are leads, not proof.
2. `source-mapped`: record official document URL, title, exact pinpoint, kind, revision, and actual date checked. The tool checks required fields, not whether the source supports the claim.
3. `requires-revision`: source is missing, outdated, contradictory, or text must be rewritten; keep it out of release consideration.
4. `reviewed-for-editorial-consideration`: record controlling jurisdiction, affected population, effective date window, exceptions, and actual named/date-stamped human review. This still does not authorize publication.

A separate eventual **signed release manifest** must require exact approved content hashes, reviewer decisions, legal/tax sign-off where appropriate, mobile/accessibility validation, source integrity, and explicit owner publication authorization. Nothing in PR #34 supplies that manifest.

## Safety / data requirements
- No employee records, client data, payroll credentials, tax IDs, API keys, approval signatures or private identity records in the public marketing repo.
- Do not synthesize reviewer names, publication dates or approval records to pass tests. Unit-test fixtures are illustrative, not evidence.
- Automated tests validate structure only; they cannot establish the truth, authority, currency or legal applicability of statements.
- The claim ID uses a non-cryptographic content fingerprint for editorial matching, not authentication or tamper-evident evidence. A revised claim intentionally gets a different ID. Release manifests will need a cryptographic hash generated by controlled CI.
- Proposed or announced 2027 rules must be clearly separate from final published, effective 2027 law.
- Quarterly reviews are research proposals, not automatic publication.

## Blocking before release
- Full individual claim/source reconciliation and qualified review across all 16 candidate pieces.
- State and local agency overlays and international applicability assessment.
- Versioned effective dates for each legal assertion.
- Accessibility, mobile and security review.
- Exact-head CI and Vercel results.
- Explicit approval to remove `noindex`, add navigation, merge and publish.

PR #34 stays a draft. All initial claim review records remain `needs-source`. Publication is HOLD.

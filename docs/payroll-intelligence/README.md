# Payroll Intelligence 2026–2027 — editorial and release contract

Status: DRAFT — no publication approval, no deployment approval, no product build scope.

This site-only feature belongs to `signal-interface/pse-marketing`, not the PSE platform runtime. The photographed *Payroll Source* table of contents was used as a coverage planning reference only; independently authored content is required.

## Rules
- 15 directories are coverage indexes; do not call them completed articles.
- Every article needs content ID, jurisdiction, effective date or applicability caveat, primary authority, source-check date, reviewer, approver, version, corrections history and practical controls.
- Do not publish a legal/tax interpretation, an unverified future rate, an automatically drafted change or an archived quarter without a human editorial approval record.
- Archive approved quarterly editions, label retrospective backfills and record interim material changes separately.
- Do not collect payroll employee data or connect this site to CHAP, client admin or runtime APIs.
- First release is 2026-Q4 only when content/CI/marketing gates are approved. 2027 quarters are future plans, not live releases.
- Avoid copying text, tables, examples, practice questions or study materials from third-party publications.

## Publication gate
Before promotion to production: legal/editorial sign-off, citation review, effective-date review, content security review, accessibility, mobile QA, build checks, explicit site publication approval. Routes have `robots: noindex` while in editorial preview and are intentionally absent from primary navigation and site routes.

## Quarterly cadence
2026-Q4 inaugural (approval-based), 2027-Q1 target Jan 15, Q2 Apr 15, Q3 Jul 15, Q4 Oct 15. Interim notices on high materiality changes after review.

## Meaning of completion

**Complete:** 51 different geographic jurisdiction entries (50 states + D.C.), each with one dated original educational briefing, a state-government source link, a topic, a pinpoint or agency-reference description, and applicability warnings.

**Not complete:** 51 full legal profiles, all payroll taxes, unemployment, wage bases, wage statements, overtime, leave, garnishments, local ordinances, all effective-dated updates, attorney/tax certification, or client-specific applicability. Do not claim a full 50-state compliance ruleset. The standing 51-jurisdiction Final Pay workbook is still unratified and is NOT imported to public marketing as confirmed law.

# PSE Payroll Intelligence — State Source Briefings, 2026-Q4 Release

**Scope:** PSE marketing education only, no operating payroll rules.  
**Date researched:** November 3, 2026  
**Target:** `/payroll-intelligence/state-sources/[state]` (11 selected source-linked pages).  
**Publication posture:** Founder previously approved publishing original, official-source-cited educational material on the marketing site. These articles are not a legal compliance certification, complete state profile or authorization for automatic rule changes.

## Release contract

- Each of 11 jurisdictions has one narrow education topic selected from a current or agency-published official source.
- Every public article displays an original PSE interpretation, precise statutory/agency location, direct primary-source URL and separate caution covering important exceptions.
- State-source directory and all briefing routes are `noindex` while content is introductory/incomplete. No source citation is a claim of human legal/tax professional certification.
- Do not render or operationalize `preliminaryStatement` from the original unreviewed state research intake.
- The 51-jurisdiction Final Pay workbook remains unratified and is **not** published as a verified 50-state matrix.
- PSE Context Hub, CHAP, customer data, compliance runtime, and underlying platform are unchanged.
- The quarterly regulator review remains research-only; no auto-deploy or auto-approve.

## Distinct approval systems

**Marketing editorial publication** is founder-authorized as original, source-cited education. It does NOT mark PSE Context Hub SourceDocument or PayrollRule approved and does NOT satisfy independent legal/tax or employer applicability review. Under PSE’s more restrictive operational source workflow, regulatory sources are separately versioned, checked and human-ratified with audit and Replay Ledger evidence.

The public source index is not authoritative for compliance software or employee payments. The article’s last-checked date is research metadata, not a representation that every law remains unchanged on every future date.
